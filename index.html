About Me

I'm a Computer Science PhD student at University of Southern California and a graduate research assistant at Signal Analysis and Interpretation Lab (SAIL), under the supervision of Professor Shrikanth Narayanan. I also collaborate closely with Professor Sai Praneeth Karimireddy. Previously, I did my Bachelors in the ECE Department at National Technical University of Athens where I worked with Professor Petros Maragos. Also, I was an external collaborator of the Machine Learning Lab at University of Texas at Austin, where I worked with Professor Alex Dimakis.

Research Interests

My research lies at the intersection of applied and theoretical machine learning, developing systems that learn efficiently, generalize across modalities, and remain trustworthy in real-world deployment.
